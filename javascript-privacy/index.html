1. Introduction

This policy applies to JavaScript: Master the Code (the "App"), developed and operated by Abdelhakim MAHFOUD (newHMapps) ("we"). We value your trust and put your privacy first. Our principle is simple: your learning should be private and secure. When you open the App for the first time, we will ask you to read this policy and agree to it.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, process or share any personal information.

  • No registration or account is required.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, photos or files.
  • We do not collect device identifiers or any tracking information.

3. Local data storage

Your learning progress, including the 90 interactive lessons you have completed, your XP points and your achievement badges, is stored only on your device using local storage (AsyncStorage). We cannot access this data, and it is never sent to any server or cloud service.

4. Third parties

The App does not contain any third-party SDK, advertising network or tracking tool that collects user data. We do not sell or exchange any information with third parties.

5. Children

The App is an educational tool suitable for learners of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not collect any information from children.

6. Your choices

You can clear the App data in your device settings or uninstall the App at any time. All data stored on your device will then be deleted.

7. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.
