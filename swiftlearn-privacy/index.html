1. Overview

SwiftLearn does not collect or store your personal data on our servers.

This Privacy Policy applies to the SwiftLearn mobile application (the "App"), developed and operated by Abdelhakim MAHFOUD (newHMapps) ("we"). SwiftLearn is a learning app for the Swift programming language, with tutorials, exercises and quizzes.

To keep the educational content free, the App shows advertisements provided by a third-party advertising SDK (Google AdMob). When you open the App for the first time, we ask you to read and accept this policy. Before you accept, the advertising SDK is not started and no advertising-related data is collected.

2. Information collected

Collected directly by us: none. The App has no registration or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Collected automatically by the advertising SDK (only after you accept this policy):

  • Device identifiers: advertising ID and similar software identifiers.
  • Device and network information: device model, operating system version, language, IP address and network type; approximate location derived from the IP address.
  • Ad interaction and diagnostics: ad impressions and clicks, crash logs and performance data.

3. Local data storage

Your learning progress is stored only on your device (AsyncStorage): lesson completion status, quiz scores, learning streaks, XP points, challenge completion status and theme preferences. This data is never sent to us. You can reset it in the Statistics screen, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Third-party SDK

The following third-party SDK is used in the App for displaying advertisements. It collects information under its own privacy policy:

SDKProviderPurposeInformation involvedPrivacy policy
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)Google LLCDisplaying advertisements Advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interaction data Link

You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

5. Security

Data exchanged between the App and the advertising SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14 (under 13 in the United States, under 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Your choices

  • You may decline this policy; the App will then close and no advertising data is collected.
  • You may reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • You may clear the App data or uninstall the App at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.

一、概述

SwiftLearn 不会在我们的服务器上收集或存储您的个人数据。

本隐私政策适用于由 Abdelhakim MAHFOUD (newHMapps)（以下称“我们”）开发并运营的 SwiftLearn 移动应用（以下称“本应用”）。SwiftLearn 是一款 Swift 编程语言学习应用，提供教程、练习和测验。

为了让学习内容保持免费，本应用会展示由第三方广告 SDK（Google AdMob）提供的广告。首次打开本应用时，我们会提示您阅读并同意本政策。在您同意之前，广告 SDK 不会启动，也不会收集任何与广告相关的数据。

二、收集的信息

由我们直接收集的信息：无。本应用无需注册或登录。我们不会收集您的姓名、电子邮箱、手机号码、通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风或精确位置。

由广告 SDK 自动收集的信息（仅在您同意本政策后）：

  • 设备标识符：广告标识符及类似的软件标识符。
  • 设备与网络信息：设备型号、操作系统版本、系统语言、IP 地址和网络类型；根据 IP 地址推断的大致位置。
  • 广告交互与诊断信息：广告展示和点击、崩溃日志及性能数据。

三、本地数据存储

您的学习进度仅保存在您的设备本地（AsyncStorage），包括课程完成状态、测验成绩、连续学习天数、经验值（XP）、挑战完成状态和主题偏好。这些数据不会发送给我们。您可以在“统计”页面中重置，或通过卸载本应用将其删除。
